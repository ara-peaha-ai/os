For real-estate construction companies and property developers in Latin America, we offer crypto-to-local-fiat settlement in both **custodial** and **self-custodial** modes. Select your country to view the cost simulator and required documents.

Both systems are built on battle-tested open source products such as **BTCPay Server** and its plugins, complemented by a custom frontend. We assist merchants through onboarding and provide ongoing compliance support with the intermediary services that form part of the rail.

We offer **24/7 support to the end customer** for payment execution, and to the merchant technical, operational, and compliance assistance throughout the entire reception process according to the required final settlement. BTC, USDT, and USDC are supported by default.

## Why we built this

Crypto-to-fiat settlement was not part of our initial roadmap. We saw it as a legacy-type service in an already crowded space — and largely still do.

What changed was listening. Potential clients pushed back: they had real needs that generalist implementations were not addressing. We decided to build a tailor-made solution for industries with specific compliance, operational, and onboarding requirements — starting with real estate, a sector that presents significant investment opportunities in Latin America for foreign buyers, but with other verticals already in mind, such as the adult entertainment industry, where discretion and cross-border payment flexibility are equally critical differentiators.

We also see growing structural potential in this space. Stablecoin adoption is accelerating, as confirmed by [Chainalysis stats and projections](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/stablecoin-utility-future-of-payments/), pointing to a market with substantial and expanding opportunities.
