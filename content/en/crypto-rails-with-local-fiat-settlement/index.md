---
title: Crypto rails with local fiat settlement
---

For construction companies in Latin America, we offer crypto-to-local-fiat settlement in both **custodial** and **self-custodial** modes. Select your country to view the cost simulator and required documents.

Both systems are built on battle-tested open source products such as **BTCPay Server** and its plugins, complemented by a custom frontend. We assist merchants through onboarding and provide ongoing compliance support with the intermediary services that form part of the rail.

We offer **24/7 support to the end customer** for payment execution, and to the merchant technical, operational, and compliance assistance throughout the entire reception process according to the required final settlement. BTC, USDT, and USDC are supported by default.
