For businesses in Latin America that receive local fiat payments and need on-chain settlement, Fiat2Chain provides a fully managed service in both **custodial** and **self-custodial** modes.

Both systems are built on battle-tested open source products such as **BTCPay Server** and its plugins, complemented by a custom frontend. We assist merchants through onboarding and provide ongoing compliance support with the intermediary services that form part of the rail.

We offer **24/7 support to the end customer** for payment execution, and to the merchant technical, operational, and compliance assistance throughout the entire conversion process. BTC, USDT, and USDC are supported by default.

## Current availability

Paraguay is currently active. Other LATAM markets are coming soon.
