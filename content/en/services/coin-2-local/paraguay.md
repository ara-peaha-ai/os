#### Primary use case: construction companies in Paraguay

The simulator above shows live payout estimates for a Paraguayan construction company or real estate business receiving a payment in Bitcoin, USDT, or USDC from an international buyer.

The first active case was processed with [Doomo Bienes Raíces](https://doomobr.com/PY): USD 12.5k in USDC, settled locally at around 0.5% total cost, in a few hours, with no custody of client funds.

Current outreach targets approximately 2,500 Paraguayan construction companies.

| Cash | USD / PYG | None | Fastest, no docs required |
| Bank PY | USD / PYG | Standard | EAS docs required |
| Bank PY (enhanced) | USD / PYG | Enhanced | Source-of-funds documentation |

---

#### For bank PY settlement

- Passport of each partner
- Personal proof of address, issued less than 3 months ago
- Commercial proof of address for the EAS, issued less than 3 months ago
- Registration and Enrollment Certificate of the EAS (DGPEJBF)
- Articles of Incorporation including Single Registration Form and Corporate Bylaws
- Share Registry Book of the EAS

#### For cash settlement

No documentation is typically required. Requirements confirmed case by case.

#### Deferred settlement for incoming Paraguay residents

For buyers in the process of obtaining Paraguay residency, P2Pagos structures a deferred settlement option:

1. Buyer transfers Bitcoin or stablecoins — property is reserved.
2. Fiat settlement to the seller executes upon confirmation of Paraguay residency.

Available as part of the Coin2Property early-adopter program. Purchases from USD 50,000 to USD 500,000.

**What this is:** A legitimate transaction timing structure. All obligations remain fully in force.

**What this is not:** A way to defer, reduce, or avoid any declaration or legal obligation.
