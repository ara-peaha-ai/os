Coin2Property is a specialized Coin2Local product for real estate buyers who hold Bitcoin, USDT, USDC, ETH, or other digital assets and want to buy property in Paraguay or other emerging LATAM markets.

It is designed for early adopters who can pay with Bitcoin or stablecoins, even when the seller, real estate agent, or property platform only accepts fiat.

The objective is simple:

```txt
Bitcoin / USDT / USDC / long-term digital assets → property purchase in Paraguay and emerging LATAM markets
```

## Who it is for

Coin2Property is for buyers who want to purchase real estate but do not fit cleanly into traditional banking templates.

Typical buyers may have funds from:

- early Bitcoin mining
- long-term self-custody
- Ethereum ICO participation
- old exchange trading
- early altcoin markets
- historical Bitfinex, BTC-e, or similar trading activity
- stablecoin treasury management
- long-term wallet holdings

Many traditional banks, brokers, and real estate intermediaries do not understand these situations.

Coin2Property is built for buyers whose source of funds is real, but not easy to explain through standard fiat-only documentation.

## What it enables

Coin2Property lets a buyer use Bitcoin or stablecoins to purchase a property even when the seller only accepts fiat.

The real estate agent, broker, seller, or intermediary receives payment in the currency they require.

The buyer pays through an agreed Bitcoin or stablecoin settlement route.

The transaction is structured with source-of-funds support, payment documentation, and a multi-rail closing plan.

The internal execution model is not public.

What matters to the buyer is:

- they can use Bitcoin or stablecoins
- the seller can receive fiat
- the agent can receive their commission in the required currency
- the payment path is documented
- the source of funds is prepared
- at least two closing channels are available before the deal is accepted

## Source-of-funds approach

For payments up to 1M USD, the central challenge is not only settlement.

The source of funds must be understandable.

Depending on the buyer profile, Coin2Property may evaluate:

- signed messages from old Bitcoin addresses
- historical mining evidence
- wallet backups
- Ethereum ICO-related records
- old exchange screenshots
- historical trading records
- transaction history
- stablecoin conversion history
- written buyer declaration
- supporting contracts or invoices where available

The objective is not to force every early adopter into a standard banking template.

The objective is to prepare a credible, documented, source-of-funds-friendly file that a reasonable bank, seller, agent, or professional intermediary can understand.

## Fee model

The target fee range is:

```txt
0.5% to 4%
```

The exact fee depends on:

- amount
- asset used
- property country
- settlement route
- banking route
- urgency
- documentation quality
- number of intermediaries required
- risk profile
- legal structure

Coin2Property only accepts a deal when at least two viable channels are available to close the transaction.

If there is only one fragile route, the deal is rejected.
