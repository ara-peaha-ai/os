---
title: Required documents
---

#### For bank PY settlement

- Passport of each partner.
- Personal proof of address for each partner, issued less than 3 months ago: certificate of life and residence, bank statement, or utility bill (e.g. Ande).
- Commercial proof of address for the EAS, issued less than 3 months ago: bank statement, utility bill (e.g. Ande), rental agreement, or property certificate in the name of the EAS.
- Registration and Enrollment Certificate of the EAS, issued by the DGPEJBF.
- Articles of Incorporation of the EAS, including the Single Registration Form and the Corporate Bylaws.
- Share Registry Book of the EAS, or equivalent corporate document showing shareholders and the number of shares each holds, in certified copy and duly updated.

#### For cash settlement

No documentation is typically required. Specific requirements may vary depending on the transaction amount and counterparty — confirmed on a case-by-case basis.
