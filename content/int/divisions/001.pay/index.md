- Bitcoin and stablecoins, not everything else: the only two blockchain technologies that solve real problems today.
- We build on one of the two at a time, to reach the market sooner with fewer resources.

<!--more-->

We take an agnostic approach to blockchains.

Instead of focusing on disputes between maxis and shitcoiners, we focus on solving real problems with the most functional technology for the use case at hand.

Today we consider Bitcoin and stablecoins the two technologies that are useful to us, and everything else useless noise.

We consider Bitcoin the only truly global and censorship-resistant solution.

We build our payment solutions on Bitcoin and on stablecoins, integrating first whichever of the two requires fewer resources, with the goal of later making the solution available on the other blockchains as well.
