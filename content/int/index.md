Ára Pe'aha Aĩ is the ecosystem of applications that starts and grows businesses in a self-custodial way — without giving up commercial centralized services when they are needed.

Financial capital and human capital: the only two resources that, at least in the near future, give value to a business of any size.

Our technologies: blockchain for payments, Git and Markdown for knowledge.

Payment sovereignty does not mean depending on a single rail: every sale must be able to come from a crypto-native client as well as a traditional one, converting automatically into the currency that is needed. In the same way, artificial intelligence should be used without handing your know-how to the commercial models that would use it for their own training.
