A custodial settlement service for buyers who hold Bitcoin, USDT, USDC, or other digital assets and need to settle transactions in Paraguay or other emerging Latin American markets when the counterparty operates entirely in fiat.

The most common use case is property purchase: construction companies and real estate agents that only accept fiat. The same model extends to any high-value acquisition — a vehicle, a business, other assets — where the buyer holds crypto and the seller does not.

## Who it is for

Buyers who cannot or do not want to convert crypto through standard channels before a transaction. This includes:

- long-term Bitcoin holders with self-custody history
- early Ethereum or stablecoin holders
- buyers with complex or non-standard source-of-funds backgrounds
- expats relocating to Paraguay or Panama who hold savings in digital assets

Many standard banks and intermediaries do not handle these situations well. This service is built for buyers whose source of funds is real, but not easy to process through conventional fiat-only paths.

## How it works

The buyer sends the full transaction amount on-chain to an address under PE'AHA control. PE'AHA executes the fiat-side transaction — property acquisition, asset purchase, or transfer — and documents the full chain of custody.

The internal execution structure is not public. What matters to the buyer:

- they pay in Bitcoin or stablecoins
- the counterparty receives the required fiat amount
- the source of funds is documented and prepared
- at least two viable settlement channels are confirmed before the deal is accepted
- if no viable channel exists, the deal is not accepted

## Source of funds

Depending on the buyer profile, documentation may include:

- signed messages from Bitcoin addresses
- historical mining or self-custody records
- wallet history
- Ethereum-era transaction records
- old exchange records
- stablecoin conversion history
- written declarations and supporting contracts

The objective is not to fit every case into a standard banking template. It is to prepare a credible, documented file that a bank, notary, seller, or agent can understand and process.

## Fee model

Target range: 0.5% to 4%.

The exact fee depends on amount, asset, market, settlement route, documentation quality, and number of intermediaries required.

Deals are only accepted when at least two viable closing channels are available.
