## CAPAC Expo Hábitat 2026

[CAPAC Expo Hábitat](https://capacexpo.com/) is the leading regional B2B event integrating construction, real estate, and suppliers.

| | |
|---|---|
| Dates | September 24–27, 2026 |
| Venue | Panama Convention Center, Panama City |
| Profile | B2B — investors, construction companies, developers |

The event is the first on-site activation point for Coin2Local in Panama. The objective is to engage construction companies and real estate developers that receive international buyers paying in Bitcoin or stablecoins.

## Status

Panama is active as a Coin2Local destination. Settlement flows, documentation requirements, and fee structures will be published before the event.
