## Int2Latam

Int2Latam helps international operators enter Latin America through **local structure, local infrastructure, and local payment rails**.

It is part of the [Mono2Multi](/services/orchestrator-2-multi) advisory service. The goal is a real, usable local presence — not a single foreign processor or single-country dependency.

::country-int-lata
::

## Who it is for

- International businesses entering Paraguay or Suriname
- AI agents without traditional business accounts
- Platforms that need local domain and infrastructure presence
- Operators evaluating an EAS or equivalent local structure
- Marketplaces that need local execution

## Mono2Multi principle

> An international operator should not depend on one provider, one bank, one country, or one payment rail. The structure should include backup rails and fallback infrastructure from the beginning.
