Paraguay is the primary Int2Latam entry market — a low-cost, territorial-tax jurisdiction with a real local presence pathway for international operators.

Int2Latam covers:

- **.com.py domain registration** — requires a local RUC (mandatory for local invoicing), essential for brand protection and local reputation
- **VPS and VPN hosting** in Asunción and Caaguazú
- **EAS incorporation and management** — Empresa Anónima Simplificada, the standard local vehicle for international operators
- **SEPRELAD compliance relationship** for the company
